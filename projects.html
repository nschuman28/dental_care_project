Our Projects | Oral Health Collaborative

Member Projects

Members of the Oral Health Recovery Collaborative have participated in the following projects.

Learning Materials

Course for Mental Health Professionals

The University of Michigan School of Social Work offers a continuing education course for mental health professionals entitled "What Every Behavioral Health Provider Should Know About Oral Health and Dental Care".

View Course

Toolkit from the National Council for Mental Wellbeing

The National Council for Mental Wellbeing developed this toolkit in partnership with a group of national experts, including members of the Oral Health Recovery Collaborative, to help advance oral health, mental health, and substance use treatment coordination and integration.

Access Toolkit

Informational Videos

Members of the Oral Health Recovery Collaborative partnered with the Michigan Community Health Worker Alliance to develop brief videos designed to improve the oral health literacy of Community Health Workers, Peer Support Specialists, Peer Recovery Coaches, and other members of the behavioral health team.
