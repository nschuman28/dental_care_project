Danielle Rulli, DHSc, MS, RDH

Co-Investigator

Dr. Rulli is a Clincal Associate Professor at the Ohio State School of Dentistry. She is also the Graduate Program Director of OSU's Dental Hygiene program and has published 40 papers. Dr. Rulli has received the University of Michigan’s prestigious Provost’s Teaching Innovation Prize, and was a University of Michigan Interprofessional Leaders Fellow.

Analay Perez, PHD

Research Assistant Professor

Dr. Perez is a Research Assistant Professor at the Universirty of Michigan's School of Medicine specializing in Family Medicine. Her research focuses on advancing mixed methods research methodology and engaging community health workers/promotoras in research to bridge researchers and communities.

Nathan Schuman, BS

Student Researcher

Nathan is a master's student enrolled in the University of Michigan School of Infomation. After completing his studies Nathan plans to pursue a career at the intersection of mental health and technology by developing applications designed to assist those living with mental health conditions. For this project he coded data and performed web and document design.
